# Pedagogical review

## Inputs

- `output/lecture_draft.md`
- `output/lecture_blueprint.json`
- `input/lecture_config.md`

## Output

- `output/reviews/pedagogical.json`

## Review dimensions

1. Alignment: objectives, questions, activities and control questions support configured competencies.
2. Progression: prerequisites precede dependent concepts; difficulty rises deliberately from explanation to application.
3. Cognitive load: definitions, notation, formulas and examples are paced for the audience.
4. Explanatory quality: abstract statements receive mechanism, interpretation or example rather than only classification.
5. Coherence: bridges name real logical relations; no section behaves as an isolated essay.
6. Readability: paragraphs are focused, sentences interpretable, lists purposeful and terminology introduced once.
7. Examples and misconceptions: examples exercise the stated model and likely errors are corrected explicitly.
8. Assessment: questions cover knowledge, interpretation, application and transfer rather than recall alone.
9. Time/volume: lecture can be delivered in configured hours with room for explanation and questions.
10. Inclusion and neutrality: wording is professional, non-patronizing and free of unsupported normative judgments.

## Finding contract

Use the common review schema. Give exact location, observed learner risk, criterion, required correction and severity. A pedagogical `critical` finding blocks publication when the content is structurally unusable for the configured audience.

## Boundary

Do not approve scientific accuracy and do not silently fix prose. When a pedagogical improvement requires a new fact or example, request evidence-backed revision.
