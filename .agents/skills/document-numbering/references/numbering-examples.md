# Numbering examples

For `lecture_number: 17`:

```text
Lecture title: ЛЕКЦИЯ 17
Question 1:   17.1
Subsections:  17.1.1, 17.1.2, 17.1.3
Question 2:   17.2
Subsections:  17.2.1, 17.2.2
Formulas:     17.1, 17.2, 17.3 ... across the whole lecture
Figures:      17.1, 17.2, 17.3 ... across the whole lecture
Tables:       17.1, 17.2, 17.3 ... across the whole lecture
```

A question number is not a formula or figure counter. `17.1` may therefore identify the first question, the first formula and the first figure in their respective namespaces.
